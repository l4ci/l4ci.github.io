Work across tasks and accounts

You add issues to the backlog and start a round in your coding agent. That session is the orchestrator: it starts workers, brings questions back to you and coordinates reviews and merges.

Each worker takes one issue in its own worktree. Mix Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode across your accounts. Account and worker setup

Already installed? Start your first round.

Check changes before merging

The merge gate checks required reviews and runs your configured tests on the PR combined with the current base. Failed checks send the work back. Repeated failures can pause an issue for your help.

What the gate catches depends on your tests and configuration. Read about the safeguards and their limits.
