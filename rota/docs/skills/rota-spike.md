Throwaway feasibility experiment on a dedicated `spike/<name>` branch that is never merged. It answers a specific yes/no/conditional question and records the question, what was tried, the findings and the decision in `.rota/spikes/<name>.md`. See [spikes](/rota/docs/usage/spikes/).
